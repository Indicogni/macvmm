# A64 load/store decoder corpus (PLAN M17; architecture §8.3; arch/insn.rs).
#
# Format, one instruction per line:   <word> | <assembly> | <expected decode>
#   <word>      the little-endian instruction word, as `llvm-objdump -d` prints it
#   <assembly>  what clang assembles to <word> (scripts/check-insn-corpus.sh proves it);
#               `.inst` lines are encodings clang refuses (CONSTRAINED UNPREDICTABLE, or
#               features the apple-m1 target lacks), with a `//` note saying what they are
#   <expected>  `insn::decode(word)` in the decoder's notation (insn.rs `Display`):
#               ld/st/ldp/stp . [s]{b,h,w,x}[32|64]   (s..32/64: sign-extend to W/X)
#               transfer registers r0..r30 or zr; base x0..x30 or sp;
#               [base, #off]  [base, #off]!  [base], #off  [base, xM|wM, ext #shift]
#               or `prfm`, or `cache-maintenance: skip` (DC/IC by VA: no bus access, PC += 4),
#               or `unsupported: <class>` (an injected external abort, MMIO-6)
#
# Expected decodes are written by hand from the ARM ARM (C6.2), not generated by the decoder.
# Lines starting with `#` are comments.
